JasimFlow v4.9 — Mobile + PDF + Auth Patch

Included:
1. Mobile quotation/invoice preview keeps the real A4/print layout and scales it to the phone screen.
2. New Download PDF button.
3. New Share button. On supported phones it shares the generated PDF; otherwise it falls back safely.
4. Print remains available separately.

9. Mobile bottom navigation: Home, Quotes, Invoices, Customers, More.
10. Quotations/Invoices, Customers and Items use phone-friendly cards instead of wide horizontal tables.
